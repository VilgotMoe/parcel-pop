Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026 · Pre-release configuration

Parcel Pop is published by Vilgot Harald Moeschlin. This page describes the current pre-release configuration. The game is not yet available on the App Store.

Progress on your iPhone

The game stores packing progress, unfinished attempts, preferences and Daily results on your iPhone. You can play offline without creating a purchase account.

Apple iCloud

The prepared app includes support for saving completed route deliveries in your private iCloud database. The service is currently disabled in the build configuration. When enabled, it will use your iPhone’s Apple Account and iCloud settings. Unfinished attempts, Daily records, settings and ticket balances do not transfer through this route-progress save.

Purchases, accounts and ads

Store purchases, the purchase-account backend and real advertising are currently disabled. Creating store or ad-network entries does not activate them in the game. We will update this policy with the enabled providers, purposes, retention and privacy choices before activating these services.

Analytics and diagnostics

Server gameplay analytics are currently disabled. Optional local diagnostics remain on the iPhone unless you choose to export and share them. Turning local diagnostics off deletes the local diagnostic history.

This website and public support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels added by Parcel Pop. GitHub processes technical request information to provide hosting under its privacy statement.

Support uses public GitHub Issues. Your GitHub username and the information you submit will be publicly visible. Please do not post private account, billing or identity information. GitHub controls its own hosting, account data and retention.

Your choices

You can clear local progress through the game’s settings where available or remove the app’s local data from your iPhone. Apple’s iCloud controls manage iCloud data separately. For help using these controls, contact support without including sensitive information in public.
