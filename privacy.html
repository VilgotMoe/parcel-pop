Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Parcel Pop is published by Vilgot Harald Moeschlin. This policy covers the game, its prepared online services and this support website.

Release configuration: Parcel Pop is being prepared for release. The current app configuration enables optional iCloud route sync, help-ticket purchases, purchase-account recovery and advertising. Gameplay analytics sharing is off unless you turn it on. TestFlight and App Review purchases and verified rewards use separate test balances that never transfer to the public release. Availability also depends on connectivity, consent, App Store products and ad inventory.

Your progress and Apple services

Packing progress, unfinished attempts, preferences and Daily results are saved on your iPhone. You can play offline without creating a purchase account. Removing the app deletes its local progress and preferences. Completed route deliveries stored in iCloud are managed separately through Apple’s iCloud controls.

Optional iCloud sync uses your Apple Account and iCloud settings to save completed route deliveries in a private iCloud database. Unfinished attempts, Daily results, settings and ticket balances do not transfer through this route-progress save. Apple’s iCloud controls manage that data separately.

Game Center and reminders

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, the game submits scores and earned achievements to Apple and can show your leaderboard standing. Your Game Center profile and scores may be visible to other players according to your Apple settings. Game Center data is managed separately from the purchase wallet; deleting a purchase account does not delete your Game Center profile or history.

Daily reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off in the game or in iOS notification settings. We do not use a remote push service for these reminders.

Purchases and optional account recovery

Apple handles payment information; we do not receive your payment card details. A Cloudflare-hosted backend verifies signed purchases and maintains ticket balances, transaction and app-account identifiers, refunds, entitlements and help/reward results. These records prevent duplicate delivery and let interrupted requests be retried. App Attest checks app/device requests and associates a device with its wallet.

Optional Sign in with Apple links a pseudonymous Apple account identifier to the purchase account for recovery. We store that identifier and an encrypted authorization token; we do not request your name or email through this sign-in. A verified help request sends the puzzle and placements needed to check the result. Reward offers and callbacks use identifiers needed to verify and deliver earned help.

Financial and account data are processed by our Cloudflare service and the Apple services needed to verify purchases and authorizations. We do not send purchase receipts or full placement histories as optional gameplay analytics.

Advertising and your choices

Parcel Pop requests ads through Google AdMob. Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) and Mintegral may bid to show those ads through AdMob mediation. Google’s User Messaging Platform shows the consent message where it is required.

Ads are personalized only with your permission. Where applicable (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent message lets you consent, decline or manage choices and review partners and purposes; use the app’s ad privacy control to revisit them. Parcel Pop also asks for Apple’s tracking permission once, at the first ad break rather than at launch. If you don’t allow it, your device’s advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not chosen from your activity in other companies’ apps and websites. Your choice never changes the game, and you can change it any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Regional opt-out choices also apply.

Personalized or not, ads process IP-derived general location, device and advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, product interactions and crash/performance diagnostics for ad delivery, frequency limits, measurement and fraud prevention. They are not a no-data service. Each partner processes this information under its own policy: Google, Unity, Liftoff and Mintegral. See also Google’s iOS SDK disclosure. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Automatic ads appear only between levels or Endless runs, never during play, and never in your first session. Reward videos are always your choice. A one-time purchase permanently removes automatic ads between levels; optional reward videos remain available by choice. To report an inappropriate ad, use Settings › Privacy & support › Report an ad in the app, or the support page.

Optional analytics and local diagnostics

Gameplay analytics sharing is optional, off by default and separate from advertising choices. If you turn it on in Settings, the app sends a random analytics identifier, UTC play days, delivery/attempt identifiers, interaction counts, assistance status, Endless scores and timings (seconds to the first move, session and run length) to our Cloudflare backend. We use it only to find levels that are too hard or confusing and to improve the game. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not claimed anonymous. Events expire after 90 days.

Turning sharing off deletes queued events and requests deletion of uploaded events for that identifier when the app next connects. A deletion marker prevents delayed events from returning. Re-enabling creates a new identifier. Offline deletion requests need connectivity to finish.

Optional local diagnostics stay on your iPhone, retain at most 5,000 events and are shared only when you explicitly export and send them. Turning local diagnostics off deletes that history. Check an export before sharing it.

Deletion and retention

Settings › Progress & purchases › Purchase account lets you delete a guest or linked purchase account, including its unused tickets and saved help history. Guests confirm directly without signing in. Linked accounts reauthorize with Apple; deletion also revokes that sign-in grant and detaches the account’s devices. Continuing to use online purchase services creates a new empty guest wallet. Local progress, iCloud saves and App Store-managed permanent purchases are separate.

Minimal pseudonymous purchase, refund and duplicate-delivery records, device ownership records and deletion markers may remain after account deletion to prevent repeated grants, preserve refund adjustments and reconcile transactions. These financial-integrity records currently have no automatic expiry. Encrypted financial backup files expire after 30 days; restoring a backup preserves deletion markers. Our service providers and advertising partners control retention for data they process independently under their own policies. This policy does not promise that deleting an account erases Apple’s transaction history.

Website, support and your requests

This site uses GitHub Pages, with no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels added by Parcel Pop. GitHub processes technical hosting requests under its privacy statement. Online game services use Cloudflare; see its privacy policy. Providers may process information outside your country. We do not claim EU-only processing.

For private support and privacy requests, email vilgot@moeschlin.com. We process your email address and the information you send to respond to your request. Send only what is needed; never send passwords, sign-in tokens, payment-card details or identity documents. Use the in-app account and analytics deletion controls where available. You can also contact us about data associated with a guest purchase wallet.

GitHub Issues is an optional public channel for bug reports. Your GitHub username and submitted content are public; do not post private account details or receipts there. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing, object, withdraw consent or complain to your local data protection authority. Apple handles payment and refund requests.

Audience and policy changes

Parcel Pop is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not request contacts, photos, microphone access or precise GPS location. We update this page when enabled features or data handling change. The date above identifies this version.
