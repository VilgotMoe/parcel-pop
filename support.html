Parcel Pop support

For help with the game, purchases or privacy, email vilgot@moeschlin.com.

Email support

You can also report a bug publicly on GitHub. Keep account and purchase information private.

What to include

  • Delivery number or Daily date.
  • App version, iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What happened and the steps that caused it.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Remove personal information first.

GitHub issues are public. Never post passwords, verification codes, receipts, Apple identity tokens, payment details or personal account information. Do not upload a diagnostic export without checking its contents.

Report an ad

Saw an ad that seemed inappropriate, misleading or not suitable for all ages? Email vilgot@moeschlin.com with the subject “Ad report”, roughly when you saw it, what it advertised, and a screenshot if you have one (remove personal information first). We review each report and can block the advertiser or ad category in our ad settings.

Purchases and rewards

The release configuration enables help-ticket purchases and automatic-ad removal. Use Restore Purchases for permanent purchases and Retry delivery check for an interrupted ticket delivery. Do not buy again solely because a response was delayed.

Apple handles payment and refund requests: request help from Apple. Use Apple’s private support channel for billing information.

TestFlight and App Review use Apple’s separate test purchase environment. Test purchases and verified rewards stay in separate test balances that do not transfer to the public release. Automatic ads require an eligible break, consent where required and available ad inventory. Gameplay remains available when no ad can be loaded.

Privacy requests

To delete a guest or linked purchase account, go to Settings › Progress & purchases › Purchase account › Delete account. Turning off Share gameplay analytics under Settings › Privacy & support requests deletion of uploaded gameplay analytics when connected. For other privacy requests, email vilgot@moeschlin.com. Do not post private identity or account records in public issues.
