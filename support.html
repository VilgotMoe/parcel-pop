Parcel Pop support

Report a packing problem, ask a question or suggest an improvement using our public GitHub support page.

Open a support issue

What to include

  • Delivery number or Daily date.
  • App version, iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What happened and the steps that caused it.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Remove personal information first.

GitHub issues are public. Never post passwords, verification codes, receipts, Apple identity tokens, payment details or personal account information. Do not upload a diagnostic export without checking its contents.

Report an ad

Saw an ad that seemed inappropriate, misleading or not suitable for all ages? Open a support issue with the words “Ad report”, roughly when you saw it, what it advertised, and a screenshot if you have one (remove personal information first). We review each report and can block the advertiser or ad category in our ad settings.

Purchases and rewards

Parcel Pop has not launched. Purchases and real ads are currently disabled in the prepared build. When enabled, use Restore Purchases for permanent purchases and Retry delivery check for an interrupted ticket delivery. Do not buy again solely because a response was delayed.

Apple handles payment and refund requests: request help from Apple. Use Apple’s private support channel for billing information.

Privacy requests

Use the in-app deletion controls for a linked purchase account or optional analytics when those services are enabled. A public support issue may ask for guidance, but must not include private identity or account records.
